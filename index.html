Robert Franklin Lewis

October 14, 1936 — November 16, 2026

Robert Franklin Lewis, a devoted husband and father, accomplished attorney, and founder of the law firm Lewis Brisbois Bisgaard & Smith, passed away peacefully at home on November 16, 2026. He was 90 years old.

Bob was born in Los Angeles on October 14, 1936, the youngest of four children of Roy Glen Lewis and Isabel Nelson Lewis. He lived a remarkable life, defined by his love for his family, his friendships, and a legal career that spanned more than six decades.

A proud Bruin, Bob graduated from UCLA in 1958 and went on to earn his law degree from the UCLA School of Law in 1961. He began practicing law in California in 1962 and built a distinguished career as a trial attorney.

In 1979, Bob and six other attorneys founded what would become Lewis Brisbois Bisgaard & Smith. What began as a small Los Angeles law firm grew under Bob’s leadership into one of the largest law firms in the country, with offices across the United States and more than a thousand attorneys. Bob served as the firm’s longtime chairman and remained deeply involved with the firm for more than four decades before retiring in 2025.

Throughout his career, Bob was active in numerous professional organizations, including the American Board of Trial Advocates, the International Association of Defense Counsel and the American Bar Association, and served as a lawyer delegate to the Ninth Circuit Judicial Conference. He also lectured and wrote on issues involving corporate officers and directors, securities law and federal practice.

As proud as Bob was of what he built professionally, his family knew that his life was about much more than work. He loved being surrounded by the people closest to him, telling stories, keeping up with old friends and, of course, following UCLA athletics.

Just one month before his passing, more than 100 family members and friends gathered to celebrate Bob’s 90th birthday. He spent the evening visiting with people from every chapter of his life, sharing stories and enjoying having everyone together. He had a wonderful time, and the night brought him tremendous happiness.

Bob leaves behind his wife of 46 years, Josephine “Jo” Lewis, and his sons Craig; Steve (Sharon); Doug (Tamaris); Tommy (Ashley); and Danny (Andrew). He also leaves 11 grandchildren, Ryan (Kathleen), James, Christina, Matt, Will, Alexa, Luke, Hunter, Taylor, Eva and Nora; a great-grandson, Levi; and many extended family members, dear friends and colleagues. He was preceded in death by his parents and his siblings Roy, Glenn and Carolyn.

He will be remembered not only for the extraordinary professional legacy he created, but for the life he shared with the people who loved him.

A celebration of Bob’s life will be held on Tuesday, January 12, 2027, at 12:00 p.m. at the California Club, 538 S. Flower St., Los Angeles. In lieu of flowers, please consider making a donation in Bob’s name to the Wooden Athletic Fund at UCLA.
